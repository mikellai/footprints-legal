Footprints Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026 | Last updated: September 25, 2026

CHIAHSIN LAI (“we” or “us”) values your privacy. This policy explains how the Footprints mobile application (the “App”) handles information about your locations, daily footprints, journal and photos.

In short: the App has no accounts, and we operate no servers of our own. Your records are kept on your phone, and we do not receive them.

1. Information the App Processes

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App processes the following on your device:

  • Location information: latitude and longitude, time of recording, horizontal accuracy, altitude, speed and heading, as well as the stays, routes and visited places derived from this data.
  • Content you create: place names, tags, journal entries, trips and the photos you add.
  • Permission status: the current level of your location and photo permissions, used to determine which features can work.
  • Trial and purchase status: the start date of your free trial, and whether and when you purchased “Unlock Full Version”, used to determine which features are currently available. This information is kept on your phone.
  • Feedback: we receive the content of an email only when you choose to send one to us. The email is pre-filled with the App version, system version and device model, which you may edit or delete before sending.

The App does not collect your identity, device identifiers, contacts, calendars or any data from other apps.

2. Purposes of Use

The information above is used only to provide the features you use: recording your daily footprints, showing visited places and routes on the map, writing journal entries, attaching photos, generating recaps and statistics, determining your free trial period and purchase status, and troubleshooting problems you report to us. Every step of turning location points into stays, movements and places takes place on your phone.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your records are kept on your phone, in the App’s own database. The App has no accounts or cloud sync; we do not operate servers that receive or store this data, and we do not sell or provide it to any third party.

Information leaves your phone in the following cases:

  • Backups you share yourself: “Back Up to Phone” packages your data into a single file saved in the App’s folder (visible in the Files app). Whether to share it, and with whom, is entirely up to you.
  • Map tiles: maps are drawn by Apple Maps, so the area you are viewing is sent to Apple. What is sent is the visible map area, not your visit history.
  • Address lookup: the App sends a single coordinate to Apple’s system map service to obtain the city, district and country. This happens only once, when you create a place yourself. Unnamed places created by automatic recording are not looked up on their own. Only that one coordinate is sent, without any time or other places; the result is stored on your phone, and the same place is not looked up again.
  • App Store purchases: when you tap “Unlock” or “Restore Purchases”, the App connects to the App Store through iOS to process the payment or look up your purchase history. Each time the App starts, it also checks through iOS’s purchase service (StoreKit) whether the current Apple ID has made the purchase. These processes are handled by Apple, and none of your records are sent.
  • iPhone system backups: if you turn on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the App’s data in that backup, which Apple or your computer then keeps according to its settings.

4. Location and Background Recording

The App does not ask for location permission when it starts. It asks only when you turn on automatic recording or tap the location button on the map.

The App records continuously in the background only when “Detect Visits Automatically” is on and location permission is set to “Always”; while recording, a blue location indicator appears in the status bar. Turning the switch off stops recording immediately, and data already recorded is not deleted. The location button on the map obtains your current location once, only when you tap it, and does not record continuously.

If the free trial ends without a purchase of “Unlock Full Version”, the App stops automatic recording and no longer obtains your location; data already recorded is not deleted.

You can change or revoke location permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Footprints → Location.

5. Photos

The App accesses only the photos you pick from your photo library and saves a copy within the App. It does not read your entire library and does not upload anything. Removing a photo in the App removes only the App’s copy, not the original photo in your library.

6. Apple Services

The map tiles, address lookup and App Store purchases described in Section 3 are provided by Apple, and the related requests are handled by Apple under its privacy policy. Please see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Payment for “Unlock Full Version” is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information, your Apple ID or any other account data; the App only learns through iOS whether the current Apple ID has made the purchase.

7. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The App contains no advertising, no usage analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. If we add any such service in the future, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not delete your records automatically; your data is kept until you remove it yourself. After the free trial ends, whether or not you make a purchase, you can always view, back up and export, and delete your own data. You can:

  • Delete any individual visit, movement or place in the App; during the free trial or after purchase, you can also edit them.
  • Use “Clean Up Old Data” in Settings to delete visits, movements and raw location points older than one year.
  • Use “Reset All Data” in Settings to erase all records.
  • Delete backup files left on your phone in “Manage Backup Files”.
  • Uninstall the App: all data, photo copies and backup files within the App are deleted along with it.

None of these deletions can be undone. Backup files you have shared elsewhere (for example, to the Files app, iCloud Drive or other people), as well as copies in iPhone system backups, must be deleted separately where they are stored.

9. Data Security

The App protects local data using the data storage and permission mechanisms provided by iOS. No storage method can guarantee absolute security; please keep your device, passcode, backup files and system backups safe.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children. Because we have no servers and do not receive your records, we do not collect personal information from children. If you have any questions about children’s privacy, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

When features, data handling practices or legal requirements change, we will update this policy and show the new “Last updated” date on this page.

12. Contact Us
