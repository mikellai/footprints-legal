Footprints User Guide

Hi, and welcome to Footprints! This guide walks you through the app one step at a time. You don't need to read it all in one go. Whenever there's something you want to do, just jump to it from the list below.

  • ① Automatically remembers where you've beenEver find yourself wondering "Where on earth did I go last Wednesday?" Once automatic recording is on, the places you stay and the way you get around show up in "Timeline" by themselves. No manual check-ins.
  • ② Your data stays on your phoneWorried about your location being uploaded? Footprints has no accounts and no cloud. Your records live only on this iPhone. Whether to back up, and who gets the backup, is entirely up to you.
  • ③ Turns footprints into memoriesToo tired to sort things out after a trip? A few days of footprints can be gathered into one of your "Trips" with a single tap, and "Recap" uses charts to show where you go most and how far you've traveled.

Core features, step by step

There are five tabs at the bottom of the screen: "Timeline", "Map", "Recap", "Trips", and "Settings". Every step below starts from one of these five tabs.

Feature 1

Start automatic recording

When to use it: You've just installed the app and want it to start remembering where you go. This is the very first thing to do. The other tabs only have something to show once it's done.

  1. Tap the "Settings" tab at the far right of the bottom of the screen.You can also tap the "Go to Settings" button in the middle of the screen. It takes you to the same place.

  2. Scroll down to the "Location & Recording" section and turn on the switch next to "Detect visits automatically".

  3. Your iPhone will ask for permission. Tap "Allow While Using App" for now.iPhone doesn't let an app ask for "Always" right away, so this takes two steps. You'll change it to "Always" in the next ones.

  4. Back in the app you'll see a "Not recording" notice. Tap "Open Settings" just below it.

  5. In the iPhone Settings screen, tap "Location".

  6. Choose "Always", then go back to Footprints.Only with "Always" can the app remember where you went while your phone is in your pocket.

  7. When you see "Recording in the background", you're all set! Just go about your day and your records will show up in "Timeline" by themselves.While recording, a blue location indicator appears at the top of your phone. That's normal.

Tip: To save battery or get a more detailed route, tap "Recording accuracy" in the same section and choose "Battery Saver" or "High Accuracy". To stop recording, just turn off "Detect visits automatically". Nothing you've already recorded gets deleted.

Feature 2

See a day's footprints in "Timeline"

When to use it: You want to look back at where you went today, yesterday, or on any other day, and how long you stayed at each place.

  1. Tap the "Timeline" tab at the far left of the bottom of the screen to see today's records.From top to bottom you'll find the "Daily summary", a small map of the day, and your visits and movements in time order.

  2. Tap "‹" to the left of the date for the previous day, or "›" to the right for the next day.

  3. To jump straight to a particular day, tap the calendar icon at the right of the date bar and pick a date on the calendar that pops up.

  4. When you're done, tap the "Today" button in the top right corner to come back to today.This button only appears when you're looking at a day other than today.

  5. Tap any visit card to open that place's detail page and see how many times you've been there and how long you've spent there in total.

  6. Tap the small map to jump to the "Map" tab and see that day's route on the big map.

Feature 3

Add and edit records by hand

When to use it: You left your phone at home that day and want to fill in a visit. Or the time or place that was recorded automatically isn't quite right and you'd like to fix it. You can also jot down a journal entry or add photos while you're at it.

  1. On the "Timeline" tab, tap the "+" button in the bottom right corner.

  2. In the menu that opens, tap "Add Visit".The other three options: "Add Movement" fills in a stretch of travel, "Add Journal Entry" lets you write a few words, and "Add Photos" adds photos to one of that day's visits.

  3. Tap the place field at the top to choose where you went.

  4. Pick a place in the "Choose a Place" sheet.If it isn't in the list, tap "Add Place" to create a new one on the spot.

  5. Tap the times next to "Arrived" and "Left" in turn and set them to the right times.

  6. If you'd like to remember more, pick a "Mood", add "Tags", write a line under "Note", or tap "Add Photos". All of these are optional.

  7. Tap the "Save" button in the top right corner. When "Visit added" appears, you're done.

  8. To change a record that's already there: in "Timeline", touch and hold the visit card, then tap "Edit Visit". When you've made your changes, tap "Save" as before.To remove it, tap "Delete Visit" and confirm. Deleting can't be undone, so take care.

Tip: Anything you've changed by hand won't be overwritten by automatic recording, so edit away. For movements and journal entries, just tap one to open and edit it.

Feature 4

See where you've been on the "Map"

When to use it: You want to see on a map where you've been this month or this year, or find out when you went to a certain shop.

  1. Tap the "Map" tab at the bottom of the screen to open the map.

  2. Tap "Today", "This Week", "This Month", or "This Year" to choose how far back to look.Tap "Custom" to pick your own start and end dates, then tap "Apply".

  3. To find a specific place, tap the search box at the top and type a place name, city, or tag.

  4. Tap the icon button to the right of the search box to open the "Filters" sheet.

  5. In the "Filters" sheet, select what you want to see, such as "Places Only" or a few kinds of "Place Category".When you're done, tap "Apply" at the bottom.

  6. Tap any marker on the map and a small card for that place slides up, telling you how many times you've been there and how long you've stayed in total.

  7. Tap "View Place Details" to see every visit to this place, or tap "View That Day's Timeline" to jump back to that day's records.Pull the card up to see recent visits and photos.

  8. Tap the locate button at the top right and the map moves to where you are now and shows a blue dot.The middle one is "Map Layers", where you can switch to styles such as "Satellite".

Feature 5

Add and tidy up places

When to use it: Automatic recording found a new place but it has no name, the same shop got recorded as two places, or you'd like to set up "Home" and "Work" ahead of time.

  1. On the "Map" tab, move the map to the spot you want to add and tap the "+" button on the right.You can also just touch and hold that spot on the map.

  2. Type the place's name in the "Name" field, for example "Home" or "Corner Café".The coordinates are filled in for you, so there's no need to enter them.

  3. Choose a "Category", then tap "Create Place". The place now appears on the map.

  4. To name places that were found automatically: go to the "Settings" tab and tap "Unnamed places" in the "Location & Recording" section.

  5. Tap one of the places, type a name, and save. Once a place has a name, it disappears from this list.

  6. To merge duplicate places: in "Settings", tap "Possible duplicate places" in the "Location & Recording" section.This row only appears when "Overlapping place alerts" is on and there really are places that look like duplicates.

  7. Tap the pair that looks like the same place.

  8. In the "Which One to Keep?" sheet, tap the one you want to keep. Visits from both are kept and gathered under that one place.If they really are two different places, tap "These Are Different Places" and you won't be asked about this pair again.

Feature 6

Create and organize "Trips"

When to use it: You're back from a trip abroad or a getaway and want to gather those days of footprints, photos, and thoughts into a travel journal.

  1. Tap the "Trips" tab at the bottom of the screen.

  2. If you see a "Turn these days into a trip?" suggestion card, tapping "Create trip" is the quickest way.If you don't want the suggestion, tap "Dismiss".

  3. If there's no suggestion card, or you'd rather start from scratch, tap the "+" button in the top right corner.

  4. Type a "Trip name", then tap "Dates" to choose the days you left and came back.A trip needs a name before it can be saved.

  5. To round out the trip, you can tap "Choose a cover" and add "Travel companions" and "Tags". All of these can be filled in later.

  6. In the "Visits to include" section, check the visits you want in this trip, or just tap "Select all".

  7. Tap "Save". When "Trip created" appears, you're done.If you're not finished organizing it yet, you can turn on the "Keep in Drafts and finish later" switch first.

  8. Back on the "Trips" tab, tap the trip card you just created to see where you went each day.

  9. Tap "Write about this day" at the bottom of a day's card to note down that day's "Highlight", your mood, and your thoughts.

  10. In the "Album" section, tap "See all" to view every photo, and use "Add photos" to put in more.Touch and hold a photo to "Set as cover" or "Remove from this trip" (the photo itself isn't deleted).

  11. Tap the "⋯" button in the top right corner to "Edit trip", "Mark as draft", "Archive trip", or "Delete trip".Deleting a trip only removes what you organized. The visits and photos are all still there.

Feature 7

See your stats in "Recap"

When to use it: You're curious where you went most this month, how far you traveled in total, or how much time you spent commuting.

  1. Tap the "Recap" tab in the very middle of the bottom of the screen.

  2. Tap "Day", "Week", "Month", or "Year" to choose how long a period to look at.Tap "Custom" to pick a period of your own.

  3. Scroll down and go through the charts one by one: how far you traveled, which places you visit most, how you usually get around, how your mood has changed, and more.

  4. To see your commute time: tap the "Commute time" card and it takes you straight to "Settings".

  5. Turn on the "Commute tracking" switch, then tap "Home", which appears below it.

  6. Pick your home from the list. Then tap "Work/School" and choose a place the same way.If it isn't in the list, create the place first by following Feature 5. "Commute days" lets you choose which days of the week count as commuting.

  7. Go back to the "Recap" tab and the "Commute time" card will chart how long each commute took.

Feature 8

Back up and restore

When to use it: You're about to switch phones, need to reinstall the app, or simply want a safety net for memories that matter. Because your data lives only on this phone, backing up regularly is a good idea.

  1. Go to the "Settings" tab and tap "Back up to phone" in the "Backup" section.

  2. Choose "Without photos" or "With photos".If you're switching phones, go with "With photos" so your photos come back too when you restore.

  3. Tap "Create Backup" and wait for "Creating…" to finish. When you see "Saved to the Footprints folder", the backup is ready.

  4. Next, tap "Manage backup files" to send the backup somewhere other than this phone.

  5. Tap the share icon to the right of the backup to save the file to iCloud Drive, AirDrop it to your computer, or send it to yourself.This step matters: the backup file is stored only on this phone to begin with. If the phone is lost or the app is removed, the file goes with it.

  6. When it's time to restore: in "Settings", tap "Restore from backup" in the "Backup" section.

  7. In the file picker, find and tap your Footprints backup file.

  8. Once you've checked that the date and number of records look right, tap "Restore". When you see "Restored N records" (N being the number of records), you're done.

Please note: Restoring replaces everything currently on the phone. It doesn't add the two together. If the phone already has new records on it, make a separate backup of those first.

Feature 9

Unlock the full version and restore purchases

When to use it: Your 30-day free trial is about to end and you want automatic recording to keep going. Or you've switched phones or reinstalled the app and want to get back the full version you already bought.

  1. Go to the "Settings" tab and tap "Unlock Full Version" in the "Full Version" section.When your trial is about to end, a reminder also appears at the top of the screen. Tapping "Unlock" on the reminder takes you to the same page.

  2. After reading through the details, tap the "Unlock for $X.XX (one-time)" button near the top.The actual price is whatever your screen shows. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it never renews automatically.

  3. Follow your iPhone's instructions to confirm the payment (double-click the side button, or use Face ID / Touch ID).

  4. When you see "The full version is unlocked", you're done, and automatic recording keeps running.

  5. If you bought it before and have switched phones or reinstalled the app: tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the same screen. You won't have to pay again.Make sure the phone is signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Tip: "Restore Purchases" only brings back the unlock. It doesn't bring back your records. To move your records, use backup and restore in Feature 8.

Feature 10

Change the look and display options

When to use it: You want to switch to a dark screen, show distances in miles, or make the map look different.

  1. Go to the "Settings" tab, scroll down to the "Display & Map" section, and tap "Appearance".

  2. Choose "System", "Light", or "Dark" and the screen changes right away.

  3. Tap "Distance unit" to switch between "km" and "mi".

  4. Tap "Map markers" to decide how markers appear on the map.If you have a lot of places, "Group automatically" gathers nearby markers together so the map looks tidier.

  5. Tap the card at the top of "Settings" to enter "Your name".Your name is shown only on this phone and is never uploaded.

FAQ

Q1: Is my location data uploaded anywhere?

No. Footprints has no accounts and no cloud sync, so your records are kept only on this phone. Data leaves the phone only when you yourself tap "Back up to phone" and share the file. For the full details, see "Settings" → "Help & FAQ" in the app, or the Privacy Policy.

Q2: Why aren't records showing up in "Timeline" automatically?

Check these three things in order:

① Whether "Settings" → "Location & Recording" → "Detect visits automatically" is turned on.
② Whether location permission is set to "Always". If it's "While Using the App", nothing gets recorded once you put your phone away. Follow steps 4 to 6 of Feature 1 to change it.
③ If "Settings" says there haven't been any new location points for a long time, tap "Restart recording".

Also, when you stay put in the same place, no new records are created. That's normal. And if your free trial has ended and you haven't unlocked, automatic recording stops too.

Q3: How do I move my data to a new phone?

There are two parts. Your records move by backup: on the old phone, follow Feature 8 to make a "With photos" backup and send it out, then on the new phone load it with "Restore from backup". The full version comes back with "Restore Purchases" (Feature 9). As long as it's the same Apple ID, you don't pay again. Do both and your new phone will be just like the old one.

Q4: Can I still see my earlier records after the free trial ends?

Yes. If you haven't unlocked when the trial ends, automatic recording stops and you can no longer add or edit records. But everything you've recorded is still there, and you can view it, back it up, export it, or delete it anytime. Once you unlock, recording picks up again.

Q5: What if an automatically recorded place or time is wrong?

You can fix it yourself. In "Timeline", touch and hold the visit and tap "Edit Visit" to change the place or adjust the times (Feature 3). If two neighboring shops were recorded as the same place, tap the place field while editing → "Add Place" to move this visit to a new place. If you often get extra records from just passing by, go to "Settings" → "Location & Recording" and make "Minimum stay" a little longer.

Still have questions? Write to us from "Settings" → "Support" → "Send feedback" in the app, or visit the Support page.
