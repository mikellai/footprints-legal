Frequently Asked Questions

Why were some of my trips not recorded?

Please make sure “Detect Visits Automatically” is on in Settings and location permission is set to “Always”. With “While Using the App”, your location can only be shown on the map, and nothing is recorded once the App leaves the screen. Low Power Mode, GPS signal, indoor environments and iOS background limits can also affect recording. Automatic recording also stops if the free trial has ended and the full version has not been unlocked.

How do I change location permission to “Always”?

iOS does not allow apps to ask for “Always” directly. Open the iPhone Settings app → Footprints → Location and choose “Always”. Photo permission is adjusted on the same screen.

How long is the free trial, and what happens when it ends?

You can use the App free of charge for 30 days, starting from the day you first open it, with all features available. After that, you can purchase “Unlock Full Version” in the App. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription, and it does not renew automatically.

If the trial ends without a purchase, automatic recording stops (your location is no longer obtained) and you cannot add or edit records. You can still view all your records, back up and export them (including photos), and delete data.

Do I have to pay again after switching phones or reinstalling?

No. On your new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase, open Settings → Full Version in the App, and tap “Restore Purchases” to unlock it again.

Restoring purchases restores only the unlocked status, not your records. Back up your records beforehand with “Back Up to Phone” in the App, then restore them on your new phone.

How do I request a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment information and cannot issue refunds directly. Please go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase, and submit a request to Apple.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Footprints has no accounts and no servers, and your records are kept on your phone. Map tiles and address lookup use Apple services, and purchasing the full version and checking purchase status are handled by the App Store; none of these send your records. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual visits, movements or places directly in the App. In Settings, “Clean Up Old Data” deletes records older than one year, “Reset All Data” erases all records, and “Manage Backup Files” lets you delete backup files left on your phone. Uninstalling the App deletes all data within the App. Backup files you have shared elsewhere must be deleted separately where they are stored.

Can I recover my data after switching phones or deleting the App?

Yes, provided you backed it up beforehand. Use “Back Up to Phone” in Settings and save the backup file somewhere else, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive or a computer. Backup files left inside the App are deleted together with the App. Without a backup, data cannot be recovered after uninstalling.
